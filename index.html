Aromatic ring & substituent swap

This POC deliberately shows two different molecular structures — it does not assert there is a real single-step reaction between them.

Important: The moving colors and radii are only a visual effect. A carbon atom does not physically turn into nitrogen, and fluorine does not turn into chlorine. The model omits implicit hydrogens and simplifies aromatic bonding.
