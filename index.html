Chemistry note: The C and N atoms keep their identities. This is replacement of a ring hydrogen with a nitro group, not a carbon atom turning into nitrogen. Shown electron paths, geometry, timing, and energy are illustrative, not computed.

What this example illustrates

NO₂⁺ accepts electron density from a benzene π bond. C–N bond formation gives the σ-complex, temporarily interrupting aromaticity. Deprotonation restores the aromatic π system.
